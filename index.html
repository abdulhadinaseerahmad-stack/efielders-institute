FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which courses does e-fielders Institute offer?

We offer eight courses: Etsy Mastery, eBay Mastery, Social Media Management, AI Content Creation, WordPress Web Development, Graphic Designing, Export Marketing and Basic Computer.

Can I watch a class before I pay?

Yes. The Etsy demo class is free on this website. Scroll to the "Free demo class" section and press play.

Do I need any experience?

No. Our Etsy Mastery Course is designed for complete beginners who have never used Etsy.

Do I need my own laptop?

Yes. Students do their practical work on their own personal laptop.

What is the fee and duration of the Etsy course?

The Etsy Mastery Course fee is PKR 25,000. It runs for one month, with four classes a week and three hours per class.

What about the fees of other courses?

Please message us on WhatsApp at 0319-4436232 for the current fee and next batch date of any other course.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. You receive a certificate when you complete your course.

In which language are classes taught?

Classes are taught in an easy mix of Urdu and English.
